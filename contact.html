Simple Process

How Enquiries and Move-In Work

1. Share Your Requirements

Tell us your team size, preferred branch, and lease duration, from a 1-day pass or short project room to a multi-year office suite.

2. Get a Quote or View Same Day

Our on-site branch team sends a clear quote and can walk you through available rooms the same day, Monday to Friday between 08:00 and 17:00.

3. Configure and Move In

Because we own and manage the buildings directly, partitioning adjustments, desks, failover fibre, and generator backup are set up without outside landlord delays.

Free visitor parking is available at all three buildings during viewings, including undercover bays at 140A Kelvin Drive in Morningside. If you are enquiring about a virtual office for CIPC or SARS registration, our team will confirm the standard FICA documents needed so we can activate your address within one business day.
