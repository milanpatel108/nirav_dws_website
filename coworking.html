Common Questions

Coworking FAQs

Does the internet or power drop during load-shedding?
No. All three branches run full backup generators alongside business fibre with failover, keeping desks, routers, and meeting room screens online throughout power outages.
What is the minimum commitment for coworking?
You can start with a 1-day lease for single-day access or take a month-to-month membership. Longer multi-year terms are also available if you want fixed rates.
Can coworking members book boardrooms for client meetings?
Yes. Every branch has equipped boardrooms and meeting rooms that coworking members can book through reception during business hours.
Can I upgrade from a coworking desk to a private office later?
Yes. Because Dynamic Workspaces owns and manages all three buildings directly, moving from a hot desk or dedicated desk into a 3 m² to 300 m² private office suite is handled in-house without waiting on an outside landlord.
