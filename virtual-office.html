Who Uses a Virtual Office in Johannesburg

A virtual office removes the overhead of a full-time lease while keeping your compliance and client perception sharp. Most enquiries come from four groups:

  • Home-based founders who want to keep residential addresses off public CIPC records and invoices.
  • Remote teams operating across Gauteng who only need a physical room for client reviews or quarterly meetings.
  • Out-of-province companies establishing a verifiable Johannesburg commercial presence for tenders or regional accounts.
  • Growing businesses testing Sandton or Johannesburg Central before committing to a private office suite.

Common Questions

Virtual Office FAQs

Can I use this address for CIPC and SARS registration?
Yes. All three branches are physical, staffed commercial buildings. We provide a formal agreement and proof of address that meets CIPC, SARS, and banking verification requirements.
How does mail and courier handling work?
Reception operates Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:00. Staff sign for registered letters and courier deliveries, log them securely, and notify you for collection or forwarding.
How quickly can my virtual office be activated?
Once we receive your FICA documents and signed agreement, activation is handled directly by our on-site team, typically within one business day.
Can I upgrade to a physical desk or private office later?
Yes. You can add coworking day passes or move into a private office at the same street address without updating your CIPC or bank records.
