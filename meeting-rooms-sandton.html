Booking FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What technology and presentation equipment is included in the boardroom?
All meeting rooms feature Ultra-HD 4K presentation screens, wireless screen mirroring (AirPlay, Miracast, HDMI), wide-angle conference cameras, omnidirectional microphone arrays, high-speed symmetric fiber Wi-Fi, and whiteboards.
Can I book meeting rooms by the hour or for full days?
Yes! We offer flexible bookings starting from 1 hour, half-day (4 hours), and full-day (8 hours) blocks with discounted bulk rates for frequent bookers.
Is catering and coffee available for meeting attendees?
Complimentary barista coffee, tea, and infused water are included. Gourmet breakfast and lunch platters can also be arranged with 24-hour advance notice.
Is parking available for external meeting attendees?
Yes, we have ample access-controlled parking inside our secure 24/7 guarded premises for all your meeting guests at no extra charge.
